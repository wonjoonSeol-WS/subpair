Everything you need to study while you watch

Two lines, always

No subtitles? Translated

Missing your language? Subpair translates the main line on your computer or with AI, and remembers it.

Replay any line

Jump to the previous, the same or the next line with one key, and pause at the end of every line.

Words you can click

Japanese is colored by part of speech. Click a word to copy it for your dictionary or flashcards.

Full transcript

Every line of the episode in a side panel. Click one to jump straight to it.

Private by design

No account, no tracking. Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on translation.

How to use it

Play a video and Subpair is there. The keys work without leaving the video.

  • A Previous line
  • R Repeat the line
  • D Next line
  • Q Auto-pause at the end of every line
  • Click Copy a word
  • CtrlClick Copy its dictionary form
  • AltClick Copy the whole line
  • AltShiftS Turn Subpair on or off

The gear button changes languages, text size, position and translation for the video you are watching. Keys can be changed in the settings.

Look words up with Yomitan

Pair Subpair with the free Yomitan dictionary.

Turn on Yomitan's clipboard monitor and every word you click in Subpair is looked up at once. Or hold Shift over a word for Yomitan's pop-up.

Pick your languages

The settings page opens by itself. Choose the language you are learning and your own.

Press play

Open Netflix or your Jellyfin server and play a show. On Jellyfin, click the Subpair button in the toolbar once to turn it on for that server.

Translation, your way

When a show has no subtitles in your language, an optional helper on your computer translates them. Pick what suits you.

Offline

ezTrans

Japanese to Korean on Windows with ezTrans XP, a few lines ahead of the video.

AI API

Optional, for Jellyfin with Bazarr

Out-of-sync subtitle files? Try subsync-repair

A companion tool that checks every subtitle Bazarr downloads against the video. It fixes subtitles that drift in some parts and turns away ones made for a different episode, so Bazarr can look for a better one.
